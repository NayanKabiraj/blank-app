# "What if" idea sweep (17 Nov 2026)

Market evidence (public, secondary): a third-party analytics page lists the "What If" YouTube channel at about 8.9M subscribers and about
1.7B total views over about 1.7K uploads (estimates from a third-party tool, NOT verified against YouTube). Several sources describe hypothetical
science explainers (space, history, science) as a recurring format. Per-video views, CTR and retention for any idea below are NOT PUBLICLY
VERIFIABLE here (vidIQ credits were exhausted). Scores are my judgment (curiosity, emotional impact, visual potential, personal relevance, escalation,
thumbnail, title, evergreen, competitive opportunity, series potential), not data. Every entry is a `status: "idea"` in `research-queue.json`:
research it and fact-check before writing.

## The formula that fits this channel
Title: What if you / the Sun / Earth ...? (curiosity + personal stakes + one extreme consequence).
Hook: start in the middle of the consequence, second person ("You stop time. The first thing that goes wrong is: you go blind.").
Each answer opens a new problem (the "every solution creates another problem" rule). Label physics as VERIFIED and invented rules as FICTIONAL ASSUMPTION.
Thumbnail: the consequence, 1-4 big words, with the **WHAT IF?** tag chip (added automatically for titles that start "What if").
