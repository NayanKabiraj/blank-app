# Story craft (free rules for narration; added 26 Nov 2026)

Distilled, in our own words, from the MIT-licensed skills `artemnovitckii/content-skills` (viral-hooks, storytelling, anti-ai-writing, dumbify) and
`milad-ahmd/youtube-script-writer`, plus the scene/sequel ideas of `danjdewhurst/story-skills` (MIT). No code, no paid API. Read this before writing every plan's
narration; `python3 -m shorts.storycheck plan.json` flags the mechanical problems (advisory, never blocks a run).

## Hook (scene 1)
- Four killers: DELAY (the topic arrives late: delete everything before it), CONFUSION (needs a re-read: fewer, simpler words, active voice), IRRELEVANCE (clear but not for me: say "you/your"), DISINTEREST (no open question: state an A-vs-B contrast or a number that surprises).
- The first sentence names the topic and opens a question the viewer needs answered. Spoken, 2-3 short lines at most. Write 3 variants with different levers, keep the best.
- Banned openers: "Let me explain", "Let's talk about", "Story time", "POV:", "Nobody talks about this" (without the named thing), "Wait till you see this", "Hey guys", a CTA as the opener.

## Structure
- Connect beats with BUT or THEREFORE, never "and then". Each scene should end by raising or answering a question (an open loop) that the next scene pays off.
- Write the last line early: it must land the reframe and echo the hook (a loop). Save the strongest fact for the late middle, not the intro.
- Pick a non-obvious angle before writing (invert the villain, find the asset in the failure, jump to the second-order effect, switch the point of view).
- Escalate: explanation 50-70% of the beats, then one complication (a "yes, but" / "no, and"), then the resolution. Three clean "yes" beats in a row drain tension.
- Shorts: one idea, a payoff in the first seconds, no preamble, a loop ending. Long-form: pattern interrupt (new visual, question or number) every 60-90 s, each section = mini-hook, point, example, payoff, transition into the next loop.

## Voice (write for the ear)
- About 2 words per second spoken (Hindi: stay within the plan's own word budget). Short sentences, contractions, "you". Vary the lengths so the rhythm is jagged, not flat.
- Specificity ladder: vague, specific, concrete, lived. Aim at "concrete" at least: a number, a named thing, a thing the viewer has done today. Cut unnumbered adjectives.
- Reading level about 8th grade; the hook about 6th. Common words (use, not utilize). One idea per sentence. Teach by example. Do not turn every sentence choppy.
- Never invent a number, quote or biography (rule 4). Mark unverified claims in the research, not in the narration.

## Words to avoid (AI tells)
delve, realm, tapestry, leverage, synergy, seamless, robust, elevate, streamline, game-changer, pivotal, testament, foster, empower, holistic, unlock,
harness, "in today's", "it's important to note", "let's dive in", "furthermore", "moreover", "that said", "let that sink in", journey / ecosystem / flywheel metaphors,
and the reflex "It's not X. It's Y." unless Y is concrete. Cut an ending that only repeats the point (the closing line is added by the pipeline, rule 8).

## Rewrite checklist
1. Cut the throat-clearing first sentence. 2. Replace category nouns with an instance. 3. Rewrite "not X but Y" as a direct claim. 4. Delete analogies that fail (must be
familiar, accurate, one step, and visual). 5. Cut hype and bait. 6. Read it aloud: anything you stumble on, shorten.
