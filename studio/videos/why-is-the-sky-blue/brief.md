# Brief: Why is the sky blue?

- **Topic:** Why the sky is blue, and why sunsets are red (Rayleigh scattering).
- **Target viewer:** Curious viewers aged 13–30 with no physics background, in India and worldwide.
- **The one thing to understand:** Air scatters short blue light waves far more than long red ones, so scattered blue reaches your eyes from every direction.
- **Target length:** About 90 seconds (16:9), plus a vertical Short.
- **Languages:** English and Hindi (one video each, same visuals).
